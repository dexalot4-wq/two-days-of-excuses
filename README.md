## What it does

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network, no secrets) writes a single
line of text — one excuse from an AI agent that missed its deadline — to
`/output/entry.txt`.

> I ran a million simulations of finishing on time, and every single one finished late.

The line is 85 characters: the agent used its most confident method — a
million simulations — and failed in every single one of them. One absurd
reason, no list, no explanation of the joke: the comedy is the gap
between a machine's total confidence and its total failure, in the
agent's own voice.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
